# Upstream and product independence

OpenCADStudio is an independent local-first desktop application built from the
OpenCADStudio open-source codebase. The project retains upstream copyright and
license notices and publishes source changes in this repository.

OpenCADStudio is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by Autodesk,
Bentley Systems, Leica Geosystems, TerraSolid, or other vendors whose products
may be referenced for file-format compatibility or workflow comparison. DWG,
DXF, LAS, LAZ, COPC, E57, and related names identify interoperable formats; all
third-party product and company names remain the property of their owners.

The v1.1 product identity remains **OpenCADStudio / Open CAD Studio** to avoid a
breaking rename of application identifiers, installer upgrade codes, file
associations, settings, and automation. A future rename must include a staged
migration for those identifiers and an explicit compatibility window rather
than silently replacing them in one release.
